# Spare Parts Intelligence Website Content Plan

Company name: PartVance  
Note: This brief focuses on structure, content, positioning, and page planning for the PartVance website.

## 1. Strategic Positioning

### Brand Meaning
PartVance means advancing spare parts decisions. The name combines "part" with the idea of "advance," giving the brand room to cover forecasting, risk, suppliers, and future industrial intelligence products.

### Category
AI spare parts intelligence for factories, fleet operators, warehouses, and maintenance teams.

### Core Problem
Industrial teams lose money in two opposite ways:

- Overstocking expensive spare parts that sit unused for years.
- Missing critical parts during breakdowns, causing downtime, emergency sourcing, and production delays.

### Core Promise
Forecast spare parts demand, identify operational risk, and find reliable suppliers before breakdowns become expensive.

### Primary Audience
- Factory operations leaders
- Maintenance and reliability managers
- Fleet maintenance teams
- Warehouse and distribution operators
- Procurement and supply chain teams
- Finance teams responsible for working capital

### Product Suite
- PartVance Forecast: demand forecasting and inventory optimization for spare parts.
- PartVance Risk: part-level, asset-level, and supplier-linked risk scoring.
- PartVance Suppliers: EU/Poland supplier discovery, qualification, and monitoring.

### Website Tone
Clear, practical, trustworthy, and industrial. Avoid generic AI hype. The site should feel like a serious B2B SaaS company built for operational teams.

### Design Direction
- Clean light SaaS style.
- White or near-white background.
- Dark graphite text.
- Accent palette: industrial blue, signal green, amber for risk, red only for severe alerts.
- Product UI mockups should show dashboards, tables, risk badges, forecast curves, supplier scorecards, and part-level recommendations.
- Avoid generic factory stock photos as the main visual language. Use software UI and operational data visuals.

FAQ 8: Is this only for factories?  
Answer: No. The platform also fits fleet operators, warehouses, logistics sites, and any maintenance-heavy operation with expensive or critical spare parts.
