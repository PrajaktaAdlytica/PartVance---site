# Company Name and .com Domain Shortlist

Date checked: 2026-07-10  
Check method: Verisign `.com` WHOIS lookup for domain registration status.  
Important: Domain availability can change quickly. Before finalizing, verify again at a registrar and run trademark checks in the target markets.

## Naming Goal

The company needs a name that feels:

- B2B SaaS
- Industrial and operational
- Trustworthy for EU factories, fleets, warehouses, and maintenance teams
- Connected to spare parts, uptime, forecasting, risk, or supplier intelligence
- Flexible enough to support three products: Forecast, Risk, Suppliers

Avoid names that are too narrow, too generic, or too close to existing competitors like SPARETECH, SPARROW, Sparely, or Spare.

## Names I Would Avoid

### Riskspares
Domain: riskspares.com  
Reason: Too negative and too narrow. Sounds like only the risk product.

### SpareGuardia
Domain: spareguardia.com  
Reason: Too long and slightly awkward for a B2B SaaS brand.

### SpareLogicAI
Domain: sparelogicai.com  
Reason: Descriptive, but too long and generic.

### SpareWiseAI
Domain: sparewiseai.com  
Reason: Clear, but sounds more like a small tool than a premium industrial SaaS company.

### PartBayAI / SpareBayAI
Domains: partbayai.com, sparebayai.com  
Reason: "Bay" suggests marketplace/ecommerce, which could misposition the product.

## Final Recommendation

Choose one of these three:

1. PartVance.com  
Best balance of brandability, seriousness, and category relevance.

2. Sparevance.com  
Best if we want the name to clearly say "spare parts" from day one.

3. Sparealytix.com  
Best if we want a very clear analytics/software name.

My preferred choice: PartVance.

It gives us room to build a real company brand, while the tagline can carry the category:

PartVance  
AI spare parts intelligence for industrial teams.
